Tutorial & Workflow

Guide

A practical walkthrough for generating motion graphics in code: how to run prompt templates, align text animations with voiceover timestamps, clone custom aesthetics, and monetize.

Step 1 Text to Video

Standalone Explainer

Use this when you want Claude to research, plan, and animate a topic without recorded footage.

  1. Select a style above, such as Creator Motion Graphics or Clean Tech.
  2. Enter your topic, for example: How protein helps you build muscle.
  3. Copy the prompt and paste it into Claude. Claude writes a storyboard plan showing every screen.
  4. Review the slides. Ask Claude to change wording or scenes if needed.
  5. Reply OK to render the interactive animation in code.
  6. For vertical Shorts or Reels, tell Claude: Make this in vertical 9:16 portrait format.
Step 2 Voiceover Sync

Sync Graphics to Your Voice

Lock on-screen text and sound effects to your exact spoken words for high retention.

  1. Give your recorded video or audio file to Claude and ask: Transcribe this video. Give me word level timestamps.
  2. Copy the transcript data generated by Claude.
  3. Choose a style above, switch to With Voiceover, paste the transcript, and copy the prompt.
  4. Paste into Claude. Text and sound effects will land on the exact millisecond each word is spoken.
  5. To show your camera video, tell Claude: Keep my facecam video visible on screen alongside graphics.
  6. To test hooks, ask Claude: Give me 3 new variations for the first 3 seconds.
Step 3 Custom Design

Clone Any Visual Style

Replicate the look and motion of any channel or creator using screenshots.

  1. Take two or three screenshots of an animation style you like, such as Kurzgesagt or Infographics Show.
  2. Scroll to the Clone any style section below, pick the closest starting template, and copy the clone prompt.
  3. Paste the prompt into Claude and attach your screenshots.
  4. Claude analyzes the geometry, colors, typography, and motion rules into a new code system.
  5. Save the result: Save this design system. Next time I share a script, build the video in this style.
Step 4 Monetization

How to Monetize This Skill

Two proven business models discussed in the video tutorial for earning income:

Faceless Explainer Channels

Produce high curiosity explainer videos, like Why grocery stores put milk at the back. Generate narration with ElevenLabs, create animations with Claude, and monetize through ad revenue and brand deals.

Video Editing Services for Creators

Complex motion graphics take several days in After Effects. Most YouTubers do not code or know how to run Claude artifacts. Offer fast turnaround motion graphics editing to channels and businesses.

Audio and Recording Tips

Web Audio Sound Effects: The generated code synthesizes clicks, pops, and risers directly in the browser. You get clean sound effects without copyright or licensing problems.

Screen Capture: Play the artifact in Claude with sound turned on. Record the browser window in OBS at 60 frames per second, then drop it into Premiere or CapCut.

Production Workflow Tips

Review Storyboard First: Always read Claude's text storyboard before accepting. Changing words and layout in the storyboard stage is much faster than editing code.

Aspect Ratio Control: Claude's artifact window defaults to landscape. If creating vertical content, explicitly request 1080x1920 portrait dimensions in your prompt.
